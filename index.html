      ✓ What this proves
      
          
      For the receipt you supplied, this page re-checks the receipt's committed request fingerprint,
            signature, recorded kernel identity, and replayed decision against the verified seal mediation
            kernel running in your browser. When those checks pass, the receipt is internally consistent for
            the committed inputs it carries: the same request fingerprint and same verified kernel reproduce
            the same decision and emitted bytes. That kernel's decision logic is the subject of public Lean 4
            proofs that assert the stated mediation properties, modulo their stated assumptions and the
            axioms {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}.
